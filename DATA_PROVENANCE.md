# Data provenance

## Included inputs

NumPy-generated synthetic arrays. Generation functions and seed handling are included in app.py. Hidden true labels and clean signals, when provided, are simulation ground truth rather than experimentally measured biology.

## Reuse and privacy

The supplied code and authored fixtures use the project MIT license. External papers and future datasets retain their own terms. No donor records, private genomic data, patient images or real clinical outcomes are included. When replacing synthetic inputs, review dataset permissions and split by the appropriate donor, specimen, site, recording or family group.

## Claims

Synthetic performance supports code validation only. Literature citations contextualize a topic; they do not validate this implementation.
